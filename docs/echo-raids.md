# Echo Raids

> This document is a work in progress. It will be updated as more details about Echo Raids become available.

## What are Echo Raids?

Echo Raids are a new feature coming in the next update. At every **server save (10:00 CEST)**, one Echo Raid spawns in **every subarea** of the map.

- It replaces the spawn of a **common or uncommon** creature.
- The raid type can be one of the following:
  - **8 regular creatures**
  - **4 influenced creatures**
  - **1 Echo Warden** — 10% chance without the creature's bestiary done, 20% chance with it done.
- All creatures in the raid are the **same type** as the spawn that was replaced.

## Why this list matters

Not every subarea is shown on the minimap. To decide whether a subarea is worth scouting, you need to know:

1. Which major **area** it belongs to.
2. Whether it can be **selected on the minimap**.
3. Whether it is **discoverable**.
4. How small or quick it is to explore.

This page lists every subarea from the official [Cyclopedia Map Areas](https://tibia.fandom.com/wiki/Cyclopedia/Map_Areas) wiki table.

## Legend

- **Minimap Selectable** (wiki note `1`): part of the area is on the ground/upper floors and can be picked on the minimap.
- **Discoverable** (wiki note `2`): the area can be discovered in the Cyclopedia.
- **Scout Notes**: a placeholder for later comments like *small*, *fast*, *underground-only*, etc.

## Quick scouting tips

- Subareas without the **Minimap Selectable** flag are mostly underground or special places. You must physically walk there to see if a raid spawned.
- City subareas and small caves/dens/lairs are usually the fastest to check.
- When an area or city is boosted, all its subareas are boosted too, including hidden underground ones. Use this table to know which underground subareas belong to the boosted area.
